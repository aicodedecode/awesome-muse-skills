# Humanizer

## Overview

AI-generated text has tells: hedged openers, triple structures, em-dash confetti, 'delve' and 'tapestry,' bullet-point everything, no real stories.

Humanizing isn't tricking detectors — it's editing generic drafts into writing with voice, specificity, and rhythm.

The process: cut the formulaic, add the specific (stories, numbers, opinions), vary the rhythm, restore your voice.

## When to use

- AI-assisted drafts that sound robotic or generic
- Content that needs to match a personal or brand voice
- Editing team members' AI-assisted writing
- Making automated content feel authentic
- Learning to recognize and fix AI writing patterns

## Core concepts

- **Know the tells.**
  Throat-clearing openers ('In today's fast-paced world'), rule-of-three lists, em-dash overload, 'moreover/furthermore/delve,' vague intensifiers, zero concrete stories.
- **Cut the formula.**
  Delete generic openers and closers. Start with the specific: a story, a number, a sharp claim. End with the point, not a summary.
- **Add specificity.**
  Replace abstractions with concrete details: names, numbers, places, quotes, examples. Specificity is the fastest humanizer.
- **Vary rhythm.**
  AI writes metronomic medium-length sentences. Mix short punches with longer flows. Read aloud — the ear catches monotony.
- **Insert voice.**
  Opinions, asides, humor, first-person where apt. Voice = the choices only you would make. Add yours deliberately.
- **Kill hedges.**
  'It is important to note that' -> say the thing. 'In order to' -> 'to.' Hedging and padding are AI comfort blankets.
- **Concrete over abstract.**
  'Leverage synergies' -> 'the sales team shares call notes.' Translate every abstraction into something you can picture.
- **Keep the good bones.**
  AI structure is often fine. Humanize the surface (voice, specifics, rhythm) without discarding solid organization.

## Practical workflow

1. **Draft with AI.**
   Get the raw draft — structure and coverage. Expect to rewrite 30-50% of the sentences.
2. **Hunt the tells.**
   First pass: delete throat-clearing openers, rule-of-three filler, em-dash confetti, 'delve'-class words. Be ruthless.
3. **Add your specifics.**
   Insert 2-3 concrete details per section: a story, a number, an example, a quote. This is the highest-value pass.
4. **Vary the rhythm.**
   Read aloud. Break up metronomic sentences: short punches, fragments for emphasis (sparingly), longer explanatory flows.
5. **Restore voice.**
   Add the opinions, asides, and phrasing that sound like you. Compare against your unassisted writing — close the gap.
6. **Cut hedges and padding.**
   Second ruthless pass: every 'very,' 'really,' 'in order to,' 'it is worth noting.' If the sentence works without it, cut it.
7. **Check the opening/closing.**
   Openings and closings carry the most AI-tell density. Rewrite both from scratch in your voice if needed.
8. **Final ear test.**
   Read the whole piece aloud. Anything you'd never say in conversation gets rewritten. The ear is the final detector.

## Common pitfalls

- **Detector-chasing.**
  Optimizing to fool AI detectors instead of writing well. Detectors are unreliable; good writing is the actual goal.
- **Surface edits only.**
  Swapping synonyms while keeping robotic structure. Humanizing requires structural voice work, not word substitution.
- **Removing all personality.**
  Over-editing into bland corporate-speak. The goal is human voice, not neutral mush.
- **Keeping fake specifics.**
  AI-invented anecdotes and statistics 'for color.' Fabricated specifics are worse than generic truth — verify or cut.
- **One-pass hope.**
  Expecting a single edit pass to fix everything. Tells-hunt, specificity, rhythm, voice — separate passes work better.
- **Losing the structure.**
  Rewriting so aggressively the solid organization breaks. Preserve good bones; humanize the surface.
- **Voice inconsistency.**
  Half humanized, half raw AI. Readers feel the seams. Finish the job or don't start it.
- **Forgetting disclosure.**
  Where policies require AI-use disclosure, humanizing doesn't remove the obligation. Edit well AND disclose properly.
